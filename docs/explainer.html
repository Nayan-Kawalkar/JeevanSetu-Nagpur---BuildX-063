How that choice is made

A score out of 100, from five parts — but the score is not the deciding thing. A hospital missing a critical need is gated out, and a gated hospital can never outrank one that can treat the patient, whatever it scores. A score can be argued with. A gate cannot.

Three rules we can prove

A hospital that cannot treat is never recommended
Enforced in the ranking, and the API refuses to even send the request.
Accepting is all‑or‑nothing
If the last bed just went, the accept fails, says what is short, and holds nothing at all.
A repeated request cannot double‑book
A double tap, or a queue replayed after a blackout, is safe.
